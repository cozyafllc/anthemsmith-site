Privacy Notice

Last updated 16 September 2026. AnthemSmith ("we") forges custom songs from material you send us. This notice explains what we hold and how to have it erased.

What we collect

  • Order details you submit: your email address, the reference artist + song whose feel you want, your chosen voice (male/female), your mood, and any Instagram handle(s) or Spotify playlist link you give us.
  • Public profile material: where you point us at a public Instagram or Spotify playlist, we read publicly available captions, comments and track lists to work out the vibe.
  • Private accounts: we cannot read a private Instagram account unless the owner accepts a follow request from our own account. If you tell us to, we send a follow request; once the owner accepts, we read that account. No third-party vendor can read a private account and we do not use one for this.
  • Payment: card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

Why we hold it — our legitimate interests

We use your material for one purpose: to write and deliver your song. Writing lyrics that actually read a real person requires reading the words those people posted. We rely on our legitimate interest in providing the product you ordered, and on your request, to do that. We keep every lyric claim tied to a real source line and we do not invent names, dates or events.

Who else sees it

Only the services that carry the order: our form relay (FormSubmit) delivers your brief to our order desk inbox; Stripe processes payment; our email provider sends your finished song; and our music-generation API produces the audio. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your brief and the finished song stay on our order desk until you ask us to erase them. Payment records are kept by Stripe as their own legal obligations require.

Third-party material is personal data

If your brief includes another person's captions or comments, that content is their personal data too. Send us only what you have the right to share, and tell that person if you like — we will erase their material on request just as we erase yours.

Your rights — the erasure path

Email gabrieljtao@gmail.com with the subject Erasure request, from the address you ordered with, and name the handle(s) or order involved. We will delete your brief, the derived vibe notes and the delivered file from our desk, and confirm when it is done. You can also ask for a copy of what we hold, or ask us to correct it.

Cookies

These pages set no cookies of our own. Our host (GitHub Pages) and Stripe may set their own on their own domains when you load or pay.
